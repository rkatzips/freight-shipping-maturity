Freight & Logistics Digital Maturity Assessment

Evaluate five capabilities, view your maturity radar chart, and identify strategic priorities.

Before you begin

Identify the business unit, services, locations, and workflows being assessed. Keep the same scope for all 15 questions. Base answers on the most recently completed quarter (or the latest documented review for less frequent activities). Choose the highest level fully supported by evidence; planned improvements do not count. If unsure, verify with the responsible person. Evaluate equivalent freight workflows where examples do not apply. All questions require an answer.

Scores are directional self-assessment indicators, not a company-wide certification; the instrument has not been formally validated.

Answer all questions using the description that best reflects the organization's current, observable practices. Each answer maps to a maturity level from 1 to 5; levels are revealed only in your results.

Your maturity assessment

Each dimension score is the unrounded arithmetic mean of its three question responses (1–5); display values are rounded to two decimals.

Strategic interpretation

These results are directional self-assessment indicators, not a certification that every criterion of a maturity stage has been achieved.
